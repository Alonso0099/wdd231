My Prep Pantry Site Plan

This document outlines the site name, purpose, scenarios, color scheme, typography, and wireframes for the My Prep Pantry final project.

Site Name

My Prep Pantry will help users learn how to prepare, freeze, and organize common foods for simple meal prep. The site will provide basic freezer tips, a guide with foods that can be prepared ahead of time, and a planner to help users organize meal prep ideas for the week.

Scenarios

  • What foods can I prepare and freeze ahead of time?
  • How should I prepare different foods before freezing them?
  • How long can different foods stay in the freezer?
  • How can I organize a simple meal prep plan for the week?

Color Scheme

The color scheme is designed to feel clean, warm, and connected to food preparation and home organization.

Deep Green

#2f5d50

Used for headings, navigation, and buttons.

Warm Cream

#f8f1e7

Used for the main page background.

Soft Sage

#d8e3d0

Used for cards, sections, and soft accents.

Dark Charcoal

#2b2b2b

Used for body text and readable contrast.

Typography

The site will use Poppins for headings, navigation, and buttons. Open Sans will be used for paragraphs and general body text because it is clean and easy to read.

Poppins Example Heading

Open Sans example body text. This font will be used for paragraph content and longer reading sections throughout the website.
