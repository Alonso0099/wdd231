Prep Planner

Use this page to create a simple meal prep plan. The goal is to organize what you want to prepare, when you want to prep it, and how many meals you want to make.

Simple Prep Steps

  1. Choose a few foods or meals that freeze well.
  2. Prepare ingredients before they go bad.
  3. Label containers with the food name and date.
  4. Use older freezer items first.
